why this exists and how it works.

the problem

teachers write their notes on ipads using whiteboard software. the default quartz pdf exporter dumps every handwritten pen stroke as an uncompressed vector path. a single two-hour coordinate geometry or rotational dynamics lecture quickly swells into a 180 to 320 megabyte file.

google drive's web previewer quietly gives up on anything over 50 mb. when you are sitting in a library on two bars of mobile data trying to verify a single integration step, drive demands that you download a quarter-gigabyte file just to view page 42.

the pipeline

every night at 23:30, an automated engine checks the faculty shared drives:

  • physics and chemistry materials are mirrored server-side (zero network egress).
  • oversized mathematics notes are rendered to clean 150 dpi rasters, repacked into lean documents (15–25 mb), and uploaded back.
  • handwritten algebra and subscript fractions stay sharp on tablet retina screens while opening in under a second on mobile networks.

access model

the links on this site point directly to view-only google drive file previews. you get direct access to the lecture notes without needing root drive folders or account permissions.

disclaimer

this is not an official coaching institute portal. please do not email administrative faculty asking why their slide decks are linked on github.

maintainer

built and maintained by harman for the fr03 batch. running on arch linux, rclone, and nightly python timers.
