fr03-vault

why this exists and how it works.

archive / about

teachers export handwritten notes from ipads as raw vector pen strokes. a single two-hour class easily turns into a 200 mb pdf. google drive refuses to preview anything over 50 mb, so every time you need to check a formula on mobile data, drive demands you download a massive file just to see one page.

this archive fixes that. every night at 23:30, a script checks the teacher folders, pulls the heavy pdfs, crushes them down to ~15 mb at 150 dpi, and syncs them here. drive opens them in half a second, handwriting stays sharp when you zoom in on an ipad, and nobody wastes cellular data.

all links on this page point directly to view-only files in drive. no root folder access, no sign-in required, zero tracking, no bullshit.

obviously not an official coaching institute website. please do not email faculty asking why their slide decks are on github.

curated by harman for the fr03 batch. built with python, rclone, and minimal html.
